Privacy Policy

How PsalmsHouse Consultancy collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

PsalmsHouse Consultancy (“PsalmsHouse”, “we”, “us”) is an independent corporate training and consulting firm based in Cantonments, Accra, Ghana. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

Information we collect

When you use our contact form we collect your first and last name, email address, phone number and the message you write. We do not ask for payment details or sensitive personal information through this website.

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use your information

We use the details you send us only to respond to your enquiry, to provide the services you ask about, and to keep records of our communications. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing your information

Messages from the contact form are delivered to our info mailbox through a form-delivery service. We share information with other third parties only where it is needed to respond to you, where you ask us to, or where the law requires it.

Confidentiality and security

Confidentiality is one of our core professional values. We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect the information you give us, although no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Retention

We keep enquiry details only for as long as needed to deal with your request and meet our professional and legal obligations.

Your rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, subject to applicable law, including Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Contact us at the address below.

Contact us

Email info@psalmshouse.com or write to PsalmsHouse Consultancy, P. O. Box CT 10898, Cantonments, Accra.
