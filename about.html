Who We Are

PsalmsHouse Consultancy (PHC) is an independent corporate training and consulting firm providing top quality professional services. We are designed and dedicated to operating by a strong set of professional and ethical values.

We are committed to helping our clients to create a meaningful and lasting changes in the ever-evolving technological world.

We pride ourselves as an added-value partner in the corporate world to tackle your most important challenges and develop solutions and implement them successfully to the satisfaction of all stakeholders.

Since its inception, PHC has been a pioneer in business strategy and works jointly with clients to empower them to grow, build a more sustainable competitive advantage and drive positive impact.

We are proud to be in a global network of independent accounting and business advisory firms, whose firms share our dedication to exceptional client service.

The international networks gives us significant global reach in addition to our substantial national presence. We collaborate to leverage our skills, resources and local expertise to help our clients grow locally, nationally and globally.

Our Team

Mac Justice Brenyah

Managing Consultant

Mac Justice is currently the Managing Consultant of PsalmsHouse Consultancy, a firm specialised in Quality Management, Training and Corporate Advisory. As a consultant, Mac Justice has been responsible for client relationship management, risk management and strategic leadership for quality reviews of all deliverables.

He is a professional accountant with over 13 years of extensive experience in accounting, audit/assurance, investigation, forensic accounting, finance, taxation, IT auditing, information security, IT Enterprise Governance, risk management, implementation of accounting software and general business and management consulting. He has experience in the field of audit, tax and business advisory to several public sector, private local and multi-national companies, donor-funded projects, government and non-governmental organizations.

Qualifications
